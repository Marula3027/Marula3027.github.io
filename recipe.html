Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ☆

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 25 minutes

Yield: 20 cookies

Difficulty: Easy

Cuisine: American

Description

These soft and chewy chocolate chip cookies are an all-time favorite. With crisp edges and warm, gooey chocolate inside, this recipe is perfect for beginners and experienced bakers alike. Great for school snacks, parties, or late-night cravings!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter (softened)
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips
  • (Optional) ½ cup chopped walnuts

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking trays with parchment paper.
  2. Cream butter, white sugar, and brown sugar until smooth.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla and mix until fluffy.
  4. In another bowl, whisk flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gradually add dry ingredients to the wet mixture.
  6. Fold in chocolate chips (and nuts if using).
  7. Scoop dough onto trays using a cookie scoop or spoon.
  8. Bake for 10–12 minutes or until edges are golden brown.
  9. Cool slightly before serving. Enjoy warm!
