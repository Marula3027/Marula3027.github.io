Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of the most common questions our customers ask. If you don’t find your question here, feel free to contact us.

Do you offer custom cake orders?

Yes! We love creating custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, and special events. Please contact us in advance to discuss your order.

What are your store hours?

Our stores are generally open from 8 AM to 8 PM, Monday through Sunday. Specific hours may vary by location.

Do you offer baking classes?

Yes, we offer a variety of baking workshops for all skill levels. Check our Recipe page for schedules.

Can I order online?

Currently, we accept custom orders through email or by visiting our stores. Online ordering will be available soon.
